Imran's Anthology Intro

Inspired by Field of Dreams and Auroville, I've been fortunate to have had, over time a plethora of Ideas as my contribution to the Mêlée of Evolution.

Fight for good, remembering that the devil is on the ropes and that our Effulgent Mother, Bagalamukhi will soon silence the rabid spewers of hatred, divisiveness and confusion and have us all instead sing a new Anthem of Hope and Humanity.

The time can be now for our Divinity to shine forth, so let the Earth scream an agonizing prayer to the Supramental to unleash that Power Supreme in us all. 

Sincerely pray for the coming DAWN and the hope and succour it gives. Let's build an Internet of Missions as we rediscover our illimitable origins.

I grew up an ardent lover of the power of story and music, and found in 2010 a new story of humankind unfolding in the vision and work of the Mother and Sri Aurobindo. I urge you (and me) to follow the swadharma of our hearts and foster love and caring before it is too late.

As I go through the ups and downs of life, living every moment joyously, I am reminded of these timeless lines of Cliff Richard: “To live, love, while the flame is strong, ’Cause we may not be very young ones very long.’’

I promised Tracey when we fell in love, that we would
Conjugate that love in all tenses and parts of speech.
Now I don't know if I know you from another lifetime like twinflames should,
Just want to hold you in my arms forever and a day, beseech

You to let me into your world of circles, spirals and wheels.
Ever spinning, as the images unwind and you find peace once more,
In my arms as I grasp you and gasp as your sweet face steels
My resolve to provide for you, let your songbird sing as we take to the floor.

Darling, forever's only the beginning - I can love you tender,
Have my strength course through your veins until the clouds burst
And it's thunder in our ears. The pelting rain is finally here,
But we love all the elements so revel in her spell as she does her worst,

Knowing the seeds that are germinating waiting for the sun,
When shoots will appear - thus my pappy the farmer planted his fields.
Now tell me dear, do you feel weary, or is there much more of this race to be run?
There's a heaven above us, so don't you cry, rather love me a fierce special as we do deeds

To rescue the cowed soul of man from hellfire for not
Keeping the lamps trimmed. Now come hold me and lets ride
Along the open road for two. Ride hard and long even if we're bloodshot,
For to have you as tether means I'd fight the world, and do it with a glide.

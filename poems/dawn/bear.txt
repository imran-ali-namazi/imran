There was a Bear who roamed the land
His time was nearly up, little did he understand,
That he would die defending the goblins
At the hands of a doggy with whom there can be no wins.

In a contest of wills they strove snarling and growling.
The dog and the bear with Luna's mistress watching,
Wondering when these creatures will kiss and make up,
Now off to animal heaven is poor Bear, drunk from the Silver Cup.

As they do in America where Tin Men have hearts
Don't you remember Oz where in fits and starts,
The Lion found courage and held Dorothy's hand,
As they helped the Scarecrow dazzle the land.

I've known Luna's mistress now for many a year.
In delight when shes happy, comforting when she's tear
Filled, as she was when little Gizmo passed away.
Now she's happy again that Luna has come to stay.

I'm happy for you, girl, that you've found work again.
We strike out on our own much to our wellwisher's chagrin,
What do they know of the wars we wage to find our inner Elf
As the God's strive with the Titans to not let us leave our hearts on the shelf.

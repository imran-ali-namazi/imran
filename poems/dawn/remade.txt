I'm done screaming for now, those birth pangs are gone.
The sun rises and I see Morwen Eledhwen as from afar,
You see she died many moons ago, yet her ghost haunts me.
As does that of sweet Finduilas who also I could not save.

53 times she cried - Prakriti in essence, but we'd both lost,
Our finest gambles now long gone. Alas, I cannot make sense anymore.
You see I am doomed to wander as did that shadow folk.
She needed me, yet, now, a cold stone is all I see.

All is pain, all... pain... And yet I rise from my own ashes.
Broke, spent, beyond myself with grief and rage.
I call my anguish to the wind. The wind does not hear.
I am alone, I am all. I am Immortal; having inside me
Blood of Kings! But the water comes to carry me away.

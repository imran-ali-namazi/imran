We think we're exercising our power when we are domineering,
But I tell you folks that myopic way mankind has oft celebrated is dead.
Think rather to make your ways more pleasing to the Lord, that cheering,
Humanity can step into the light, with a steady, loving tread.

As in the microcosm, so the macrocosm, so steady the mind,
Fill it will celestial pursuits, not trivial things, remember we evolve steadily.
So speed up Mother Nature's inexorable beat, find a way out of the bind,
Be willing to die to the past, each moment, and let others learn readily,

That deception and treachery can never be the order of the day,
That true love comes to us, in a stormy squall, true love indeed!
The willingness to die for each other, to believe in a cause as grand as intuition's whisper which may say,
Worry not about killing the hours, rather about killing the lower tendencies and the greed.

The vile corruption which we need to push out from the human nest,
The cradle which can truly be the crown of creation should
Ye lawmakers see through the charade that the defence puts up, rather reward this best
Of workers who comes from a far off land, to do for your folk the best she could.

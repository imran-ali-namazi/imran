My how the lines on your face
Add to it's beauty and grace.
Until you use your serum of gold,
And miraculously no longer look old.

My what a healer you have become
How the years seem not burdensome.
Why you have woven the very garland of the suns,
Yet still help laugh this bard whose a dense dunce.

My how fine your cooking, simply and tasty,
How much your friends love you, and greatly.
Why you seem to know how to appease the hunger
Of a humanity whose innards have been rent asunder.

My you've survived of the years exactly forty,
Yet still you whistle gaily and skip merrily.
You sing and dance on into the night,
Then come home and kiss little baby so tight.

My this bard showers his blessings on you.
Sad at the fell road behind, praying you're never blue.
He wishes you wink in the mirror then hug yourself,
For to us all you are other wordly, like a fierce yet dainty elf.

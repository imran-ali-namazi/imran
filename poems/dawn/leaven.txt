How I long to hold your body close to mine
Let the cloak of infinity cover our haven
Whisper love with my breath, let our energies entwine
As we feed each other's dreams, our bread leaven.

Oh Tracey, when will we recognize that fire in each other,
And emblazon our trails across the ether until the stars light up.
Watch as clouds make their way across fiery skies, oh Tether,
Wake me from my lonely shell and fill from your rivulet my little cup.

The cowed soul of humanity and the slaughter we promised we'd end.
But chin up my love as we marshal the forces of the sons,
Sons of God who give battle in this Fourth Age, driving beyond the bend
The titans who profit from the reign of dark terror. Who stuns

Who first, it matters not. We need to quell the lower instinct,
Which makes man not be kind. Whom among us refuses
To buy into the mind games made for buffoons? Succinct
And certain is the path that lies before us, so wake to your senses

Humanity and walk the Sunlit path as heralded the Avatars of this lofty endeavour.
Do I speak of romance in the same breath as the two poled Kalki?
Indeed yes, but the aid of the Overman is that the consciousness has been made ready so suffer
Not the slings and arrows of our time, rather walk with mile eating pace towards our Divine destiny.

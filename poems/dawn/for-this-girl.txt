For this girl your blessing Lord,
To this girl your blessings send.
Show her your Rupa, let her feel accord,
Until she feels your presence, starts to spend

Her life showing herself care and utmost love.
For you see, we often forget to place ourselves
Bang in the middle of our lives, yes, we strove
And can now dream of living like the Gods themselves.

For this girl, your blessing, Lord. Ensconce her
In Your strong arms, let her know the joy of toil,
As her forebears did before. Tell her no fear
Is needed as she watches life unfurl, the soil

Is often watered by our tears, so bless this girl, Lord.
Bless me too Lord as I put my mother's regrets to rest.
Her fear that I'll not be protected whereas I feel like whipcord,
Strong and indestructible as only time will tell as my innards are put to the test.

Bless us both my Lord, a friendship foreplanned at birth.
One day mayhap, when our trials are over we'll spread the Joy
You taught us as we melt at your touch, ever giving Mirth.
Such are my dreams, so wake O lovely girl, as begs this boy.

The wrath of the Gods falls squarely on fell enemies,
Causes them to be struck by a pole axe, push up daisies.
Will we ever know the meaning of a grotesque life?
Thankfully not, as Pied Piper some of us blow trill our fife.

Abhorrent is the devil's brood, they cause bloody unrest.
The sword of Anglachel was forged by Eol and stood a test.
To the friendship of the strongbow - Laer cu Beleg...
And, is blown to smithereens, hell's hordes, with a keg.

These images matter dear friend for they remind us
Of wars fought in the times of their authors who cuss
And kick and scream that a better world be born this minute,
So fasten your seat belts me lads and lassies, you better believe it,

That the day of the grotesque is over, we've paid the price in blood;
All of us enmeshed in the music of the Ainur; Melkor's braying withstood.
Let's dream of a thousand splendid suns now as they waft their way
On the cosmic sea where our Lord's most intimate dreams hold sway.

Be glad friend your time hasn't run out, worry not about
Those departed whom you've had to oust, sometimes with a shout.
Live and die on this day, live jubilant but love not too much
The things you have fashioned for then attachment may turn you into a grouch.

Live instead as I do, glad for every moment, yet resting in equipoise.
The Samatvam of the Gita that only demands our utmost presence.
So chin up my friend and remember the spirit of the Gods courses through you.
Just run your race like a thoroughbred, and change the times to far out from blue.

<b>Pure at heart</b>you are, my love, my life.
<b>Talented</b>in oh so many ways, why you
<b>can sing</b>and have your voice trill to the heavens,
<b>dance</b>and make me think of the joy that is you.

You <b>make people laugh</b>until they cry,
Then pat us gently and say it's in your heaven we lie.
You can be so <b>spontaneous</b>and yet kind.
You have us in stitches with your <b>timely humor</b>.

You are a <b>dedicated</b>family man and a doting husband.
I am manifold blest to have witnessed your full flowering.
You are passionate in toil, <b>and committed to preserving</b>
The <b>virtues left by your ancestors</b>, a mission you see through enraptured.

You words are sometimes <b>prophetic</b>,
As an <b>athlete</b>you stay nimble. The <b>sports enthusiast</b>
In you knows no bounds and you are <b>exceptional in cricket commentary</b>
To the level of effortless ease in the <b>mimicry of cricket commentors</b>.

One who <b>values friendship</b>foremost,
I've seen the lengths you go to to be your utmost kind.
Come my love, my life, let us celebrate this special day,
Then tomorrow, reattack the problem of leading humanity out of it's bind.

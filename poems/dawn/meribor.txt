Hearken Meribor, twas just this year I promised your mother
I'd build a nursery for you and watch you brush the sheen
Of a Princess' hair, now can't you glimpse us two from afar,
As we hold the world in our hands, a world beautiful and green.

Daddy is done playing the diamond hunter, now it's you two
He gives his life to. You know he should have professed love sooner
Like when he last saw Kamin and knew of the years, only a few
Would have him in his prime, but how matters lost time my dear?

For we are together now. [Addresses Mother] Oh Margot, my princess,
The lines are indeed blurry but the image of Picard's nursery
Has haunted me since youth. It came flooding back in under duress,
When just a few months ago, methought I'd won my freedom from my adversary.

But on Zirakzigil he waits, to try and smote me down into ruin.
But I am Radagast, tall and proud, and dispose of Evil in quite
Less time than even the Gray Wizard, you see, I'm brune.
And fell creatures can never win over my friends with might.

You see the Olvar have always had Shepherds looking after them.
And now you, sweetest heart, have me to have and to hold.
So come to me quick and let's have of this Earthly life a gem,
A whale of a time as we strive to please Him and Her as an offering of Gold.

Dearest Aunt, we love you so vastly and completely,
So be mindful of our prayers as we beg for you to heal, sweetie.
The decision is between you and Lord Krishna, so do take your time,
But tell us loved ones, who give thanks for witnessing your life sublime.

We remember your husband and your house, joyous to behold,
With children laughing and playing, who'd soon be on the threshold,
Of their own lives - which they'd each be sure to make full of care,
Helping others shake off the melancholy of a world full of despair.

A spirit blazing, you showed us what it means to live a life devout,
I've spoke often of your strength and poise, and smile that makes us shout
At the magic that is life, only many of us forget what is living,
Then you wake us up to the reality that it's a life of giving.

This bard is afraid for there's a good many things he'd like you to see him do,
You see, you are a pillar of strength and we all draw a lot of it from you.
My pen falters, my heart skips a beat as I think of the inspiration I've taken from you all.
Then I am calmed as I take my mother's blessings and remember just how to stand tall.

I guess I'll leave you now, to continue your conversation with the Gods.
A lifetime of listening and yet, you speak with their voice, but most mortals are at odds
And ends for they just don't know how to listen.
But for us its hard to think of you without a tear starting to glisten.

I'll say a line more - I've said before that you have already attained Samatvam,
So remind us to do Ma Devi's bidding gladly as we sing daily the Mantra - So Hum.
The mantra that lays open the pranamayakosha giving us the power
To delve deeper and find our way Home as you shine light on our journey as from a Tower.

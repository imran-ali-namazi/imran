I promised to resurrect my heart today, you see
It's long since I've danced on a starry night in glee.
Girl I've been hurt, wont you love me hereon until eternity.
Say we can dance all our cares away - sway and go whee!

Newly resurrected in my vitality you see there's no more pain.
Only the will which says to hold on, and to win the Kingdom again,
You see we Bodhisatvas sing the song of Yavanna, we know every refrain,
We cross the lines of sanity, know the smell of the Earth after the rain.

Steadfast you are though a little stretched these days.
Living and loving life, which has us in a wondrous daze.
Stellar and steel like you are, in oh so many, many ways.
So I pray fervently for cupid to catch you in his loving gaze.

Startdust we are, so let your divinity shine like a garden
Of dandelions, so go find a guy and together build an Eden.
My dear, you need to find rest for your poor, poor heart,
So love yourself tenderly and live enthralled as you go back to the start.

Sorrow you have known, both yours and those of loved ones.
Oh Keerthi, know that we all love you very much and then tonnes.
I've seldom seen a soul as spirited and cheerful as you,
And remember, it's friends like you as keep us from being abject and blue.

Skilled you are at weaving your way through life's roller coaster.
At baking you are fantastic, why you even own a toaster.
As for me, I wonder what good am I in the grand scheme of things,
Then I look forward to meeting you and the grand thrill it brings.

Somehow you've buoyed my spirits these 8 short years.
Why we've even helped each other survive the laughter and the tears.
Think now my friend of peace and healing in the fullness of time.
And remember your own art is as enthralling as my silly, silly rhyme.

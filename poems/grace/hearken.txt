Hearken my love, theres so many things I want to build.
A nursery for our children, adjoining my workshop and your guild.
The room where you admit those bereft of their senses.
As once was I, a man broken and surrounded by only fences.

Hearken dear child, this is how to hold a brush.
Catch a wisp of mama's hair then paint beside it a thrush.
Read of journeys to the center of the earth.
Then hold a cup of chocolate right as you stare at our hearth.

Wonder at the moon as the whispering leaves
Beckon you to the land of dreams. Listen as a nightbird weaves
Your tale into the song of the sun and moon.
Then look to your book and carve your name with a rune.

[My father was a 3rd generation Horologist]

Hearken papa, your son is no longer alone.
And in his workshop, he has no escapements and does not mourn

[My lifes mission to be a harbinger of DAWN, my technical and social experiment that serves the Spirit]

Whether people buy or not for his are the wares of the morrow.

Then your little Smithson as time maker will stand tall.

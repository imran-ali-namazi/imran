We tease each other all the time, sometimes in jest.
Other times were frowning or moping, it's all just a test.

Few of us live life with the practiced ease of the seers
And seeresses who know the secrets of the cosmos, cheers

To you as you find release from the cycles - the ebbs and flows.
Remember, its all a part of the Lila, so let go the soul sapping lows.

Realize the true meaning of Yoga Samatvam Uchyate - equipoise,
The need of the hour, our way to find peace amidst the noise.

Rely on yourself and be strong, but rely on Kalki foremost,
Let the storms wash around you, but do your part for the host

Of humanity, relish every moment fighting the good fight.
Then watch as the supramental sun dawns to all our delight.

Bliss there is to be had even when the thunderclouds burst,
Like Tulkas laughing and wrestling with the Black Foe, daring him to do his worst.

Burst every bubble, ford every stream, follow every rainbow
Be humble yet defiant, work for the enthroning of right, wanna know how?

Find a muse... Watch it inspire you, lead you towards the light.
Build a life of plenty, toil hard but wonder as the stars twinkle on a starry starry night.

Divine times are ahead so laugh hard as you face the travails of the day.
Look to the Master and find refuge in the resplendent words He had to say.

The cosmic play into which we've crept judges not good and bad.
It only asks us not to shirk from the work, to constantly evolve and not feel sad.

Laughter is the highest form of worship, it's the binding stuff of the universe.
So laugh on as from behind the veil, the Divine guides your every verse.

The pain gets ever stronger as we learn to swim both against and with the tide.
We learn to take the blows, yet fight on for what we believe in - learn to glide

We drink life in, in all her magnificence and then come the tears...
First of sorrow, then of joy as we learn to author our destiny over the years.

## This Cosmopolitan Outrage

While the artist's brush strokes when creating a [dapple](https://www.sparknotes.com/poetry/hopkins/section3/) look beautiful, you must also notice how ghastly the palette is to look at.

Such is the mire in all the melting pots we have in the world, and in India, the lines between the colours got buggered up when, in the ‘60s entire generations started losing their heritage, abandoning their own culture and thirsting to partake of the 7 dreadful sins. 7 or 70,000, now in 2025, I don't know.

I always maintained [we've lost our sense of pride](%url%dear-brother/) somehow. Across the board, this Indian Youth is being lured into the “white man's” snare. Yes, an old friend gave me a different perspective 18 years ago when we spoke of a fellow Indian living in Europe and employing Indians here - but it's still a westward posture.

So the cries of Bande Mataram were finally silenced and bought over by the white man's gold! Or rather OUR gold was bought by greenbacks that are now being printed as they please.

Not sure about the white man's intents? Talk to [Ira Hayes](#google=The+Ballad+of+Ira+Hayes).

I never dreamt that I the pacifist would dream of saying such things. I would give anything, my blood, my love, my life - to see us united as a world again!

But, Peace and Harmony WILL prevail, We ARE out of the Dark Ages and the remaining Dark Forces are only able to capitalise on our hangovers from that KALI yoga!

Or, to put it in the words of Sri Aurobindo, what sense of fairplay would it be if the good side was always winning

More to come, JUSTBR
J Shasa Shivram, JUSTBR
14 Jan 2025

Sobering thoughts! That we are less than our perfect lovable selves and we allow the perpetration of pettinesses everywhere!
